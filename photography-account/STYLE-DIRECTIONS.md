# 9 STYLE DIRECTIONS — pick one, then I build the full preset

You said the first four were too simple. These are sharper, each with a real technical signature, a position in the market, and an honest difficulty rating. **None of them copy Máté, Flach or van Oosten** — each takes a different axis.

Ratings: **Difficulty** = how hard it is to shoot consistently · **Print** = how well it sells as wall art · **Distinct** = how unlike the genre baseline it looks.

---

## 1 · DUST & DAWN — *temperature separation*
**Cool, desaturated habitat. The bird is the only warm thing in the frame.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature moves | Background mask `Temp −12 · Sat −18 · Clarity −20` · Subject mask `Temp +10 · Texture +18` · Contrast −12 · Sat −6 · Green −45 · Shadows hue 210 @ 10 · Grain 22 · Vignette −10 |
| Signals | Patience, restraint, authorship. Quietly expensive |
| Difficulty | Medium · **Print** Excellent · **Distinct** High |
| Best conditions | Golden hour and overcast, Thol and Indroda |
| Risk | Easy to underdo — if everything is desaturated equally, it's flat, not styled |

## 2 · SLATE & EMBER — *selective colour, done with restraint*
**The frame is almost monochrome. One warm note survives — the eye, the beak, the legs.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature moves | Global Sat −30 · Background mask Sat −70 · B&W conversion at ~70% via Sat, then paint back Orange/Red/Yellow on the subject only · Split tone shadows 210 @ 14, highlights 40 @ 6 · Blacks −22 · Grain 26 |
| Signals | Art-direction confidence. This is a *designed* image, not a snapshot |
| Difficulty | Medium-high (masking discipline) · **Print** Excellent · **Distinct** Very high |
| Best conditions | Any light — it's light-independent, which is a huge practical advantage |
| Risk | The classic "spot colour" cliché if overdone. **One** warm note. Not two |
| Note | Directly on-trend: "monochrome edits with one accent colour" is a rising 2026 signal [10](https://www.viewbug.com/blog/top-100-photography-trends-for-2026-whats-popular-whats-next-how-photographers-are-winning-on-viewbug) |

## 3 · PORTRA RESTRAINT — *film, honestly*
**Kodak Portra's grandchild: warm mids, cool shadows, lifted blacks, real grain.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature moves | Profile **Adobe Neutral** · Contrast −18 · **Blacks +18** (lifted, film base) · Highlights −45 · Shadows +30 · Split tone: shadows **215 @ 12**, midtones 40 @ 8 · **Grain 32 / size 45 / rough 60** · add a matte fade via curve black-point lift to output 22 |
| Signals | Analog literacy, fine-art print culture, no digital harshness |
| Difficulty | Low (forgiving) · **Print** Excellent · **Distinct** Medium-high |
| Best conditions | Overcast and open shade — where digital files look flattest, this looks intentional |
| Risk | Can read as muddy on a phone screen. Keep mid-grey luminance near 50% |
| Note | "Film-inspired colour grading" and "grain as a texture choice" are both rising 2026 trends [10](https://www.viewbug.com/blog/top-100-photography-trends-for-2026-whats-popular-whats-next-how-photographers-are-winning-on-viewbug) |

## 4 · HIGH-KEY MIST — *the opposite of moody*
**Bright, airy, hazy. The bird is small and the emptiness is the subject.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature moves | Exposure +0.35 · Contrast −25 · **Whites +22** · **Blacks +20** · **Dehaze −10** (keep the haze) · Highlights −30 · Shadows +35 · Cool highlights (hue 200 @ 5) · Grain 15 · Vignette **0** |
| Signals | Fine-art minimalism, negative space, confidence |
| Difficulty | High — you need genuinely misty or backlit-sky conditions, and perfect exposures |
| **Print** Very good (especially large) · **Distinct** Very high | |
| Best conditions | Winter fog at Thol and Nalsarovar, Nov–Feb pre-dawn; birds against bright sky |
| Risk | Blown highlights are fatal. Expose for the brightest feather, not the average |

## 5 · NOCTURNE — *chiaroscuro*
**Dark field, single shaft of light, the bird emerges from black.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature moves | Exposure −0.30 · **Blacks −32** (the only direction where this is allowed) · Highlights −70 · Shadows +20 · Clarity +10 local on the bird only · radial burn on all four corners (`Exposure −0.45, Feather 80`) · Vignette −18 · Grain 30 |
| Signals | Drama, cinema, gallery weight |
| Difficulty | High — needs genuinely dark backgrounds and controlled light |

| Risk | Crushed blacks are the #1 amateur tell. Never past −35, and always keep some shadow detail |

## 6 · HALO — *backlit translucency*
**Light through the feathers. Glowing edges, translucent wings, a rim you can see through.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature moves | Exposure −0.20 · Highlights −75 · Shadows +45 · **Dehaze −6 (never dehaze the glow away)** · Texture +20 on rim feathers · warm glow radial behind the head (`Temp +15, Exposure +0.20`) · **remove chromatic aberration + manual defringe** · Grain 28 |
| Signals | Technical mastery — this is the hardest light to hold, and everyone knows it |
| Difficulty | Very high · **Print** Excellent · **Distinct** Very high |
| Best conditions | Low sun through feathers; backlit perches; takeoff against bright sky |
| Risk | Fringing. Defringe every frame, and check the wing edges at 200% |

## HOW TO CHOOSE — three questions

1. **How much control do you have over conditions?** Low control → **Portra Restraint** (#3) or **Slate & Ember** (#2). High control → **Halo** (#6) or **Nocturne** (#5).
2. **Selling prints or chasing followers?** Prints → **Platinum** (#7) or **Graphic** (#9). Followers → **Dust & Dawn** (#1) or **Halo** (#6).
3. **One look forever, or seasonal?** If seasonal: **Cyanotype** Nov–Feb (water birds), **Dust & Dawn** Mar–Jun (dust and heat), **Halo** Jul–Oct (monsoon backlight).

## MY RECOMMENDATION

**Run #1 Dust & Dawn as your base** — it's light-tolerant, prints beautifully, and the temperature differential is a signature nobody at the top of this field is using.

Then add **two series** on top so the feed never goes static:
- **Cyanotype** (#8) as your Nov–Feb migration series
- **Platinum** (#7) as one frame in every nine

That gives you a recognisable base, a seasonal event, and a fine-art credential — three layers of identity instead of one flat preset.
