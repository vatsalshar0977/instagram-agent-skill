## TIER B — MIGRATION / FLYWAY (the strongest story you have)

This isn't decoration — it's geographically true and it's the spine of your shooting year. **Nalsarovar lies on the Central Asian Flyway**, with 329 species recorded there, and the 2024 census counted over **4.67 lakh birds across Nalsarovar and Thol** [1](https://www.learnupsc.com/2023/08/nal-sarovar-bird-sanctuary.html) [2](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/gujarat-favourite-state-migratory-as-well-as-local-393.htm). Your entire November–February season *is* the flyway.
