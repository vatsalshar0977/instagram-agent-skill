# voice.md — Portfolio Bird Photography, Canon R8

## Who I am
- **Name:** Vatsal Sharma
- **Handle:** @vatsalsharma.photo
- **What I do, in one sentence:** I photograph birds in Gujarat and make prints of the frames worth keeping
- **Who I am talking to:** people who would hang a bird photograph on a wall — birders, nature lovers, interior buyers, mostly India with an international art audience
- **What I sell:** prints (link in bio), later licensing and exhibitions

## What I sound like
- **Three captions that sound like me:**
  1. "She came back to the same dead branch for four mornings. On the fifth, the light arrived with her."
  2. "Four hours of nothing, then eleven seconds."
  3. "This one is from the hide I built for almost nothing, in the dark, at five."

- **On camera / in captions I am:** quiet, observant, a little understated. I describe what happened. I don't explain what to do.
- **Words I actually use:** light, wait, perch, dawn, frame, still, quiet, hold, came back, morning, branch, water, eye level
- **Words I would never say out loud:** "pro tip", "here's how to", "settings", "tutorial", "follow for more", "swipe", "wait for it", "the algorithm", any emoji chains
- **Do I teach:** no. If someone asks a settings question, I answer it in one line in the comments. It never becomes a post.
- **Do I swear:** no
- **Emoji in captions:** none, or one at most
- **Face on camera:** almost never. A hand, a silhouette, a shoulder in the cinematic pieces only
- **Voiceover:** rarely. Ambient sound is the soundtrack; if I ever speak, it's one sentence
- **Pace:** 150 wpm, or silence

## My positions
1. The frame is finished in the field, not in the edit. Minimal processing, honest colour.
2. Light and patience beat reach and gear. I will drive nowhere for a rarity in bad light.
3. The bird's welfare comes before the photograph. No playback, no baiting, no flushing, no nest disturbance, no posted locations of nesting rarities.
4. I don't post record shots. If I wouldn't print it at 12×18, it doesn't go on the feed.

## Off limits
- **Topics I do not post about:** politics, gear wars, brand loyalty, other photographers' work criticised in public
- **Names, numbers or locations I cannot share:** exact locations of nesting or endangered species; private land without permission
- **Claims I am not allowed to make:** anything I haven't done. No invented wait times, no invented keeper counts, no "years of experience" with birds I don't have yet
- **Never:** watermark on the image (kills reposts by feature accounts), emoji hashtag walls, engagement bait
