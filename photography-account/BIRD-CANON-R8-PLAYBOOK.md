## 3. CANON R8 BIRD SETTINGS — The Content Engine

From [Canon R8 wildlife review](https://www.apcwildlife.com/blog/canon-r8-review-wildlife-photography) + Reddit r/canon:

**Your C1 for perched birds:**
- File: CRAW (smaller than RAW, same quality)
- Mode: Manual + Auto ISO (max 6400) — you control shutter + aperture, camera handles exposure
- Shutter: 1/1600 minimum for perched, 1/3200 for flight (size dependent)
- Aperture: Wide open — f/6.3 on RF 100-400, f/8 on RF 200-800
- Continuous: High speed 20fps (40fps in low light but rolling shutter risk)
- AF: AI Servo (continuous)
- Subject: Animals + Birds + Eye Detect ON
- AF Area: 1-point AF or Spot AF for perched (precise through branches), Flexible Zone AF 1 for flight
- Back-button AF: Set AF-ON to tracking, * button to single point — separate AF from shutter

**Your C2 for birds in flight:**
- Same but: Flexible Zone AF 1, Whole Area Tracking ON, Shutter 1/3200-1/4000

**C3 for low light / dawn chorus:**
- Auto ISO max 12800, Shutter 1/800, use Spot AF, turn off eye detect if it hunts

**Why this is content:** 90% of R8 bird shooters use wrong AF area. Your Reel: "This $0 setting was ruining my bird photos" = Whole Area Tracking ON vs OFF. Show before/after.

**Back-button AF explainer for Reels:**
"Factory default focuses when you half-press shutter. If you recompose, it refocuses on background. Back-button separates focus (thumb) from shutter (finger). Hold AF-ON to track bird, release to lock focus, recompose, shoot. No focus drift."
