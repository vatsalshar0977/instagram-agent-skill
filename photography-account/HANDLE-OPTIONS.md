## How to pick (the rules that matter)

1. **Handle = your name or a mood. Name field = keywords.** Search happens on the name field, not the handle. Buyers find you through "bird photography", not through a clever handle.
2. **Pronounceable.** A print buyer will hear your handle said out loud at some point. If they can't spell it back, you lose the sale.
3. **No digits, no trailing underscores.** `@vatsal_birds_07` reads like a spam account next to a gallery grid.
4. **No gear in the handle.** Not `@r8birds`, not `@canon_birds`. You will change bodies.
5. **Don't lock yourself to Gujarat in the handle** unless you're happy being a regional account forever. Put Gujarat in the name field and the hashtags instead.
6. **Avoid "photography" spelled out in the handle.** `.photo` / `.frames` / `.studio` is shorter and looks like a real practice.
